Smartech Typing Tutor - STUDY MATERIAL file (upload it to GitHub next to your exercises file, name: materials.txt)

It fills the "Typing Books & Study Material" and "Video Lessons" pages. One block per item:
  ### Book | Title of the book or notes       (Book = shown under Books & Study Material)
  ### Video | Title of the recorded class      (Video = shown under Video Lessons)
  url: https://...                             (required - a PDF, a web page, a YouTube link ...)
  desc: one line about it                      (optional)
  language: English                            (optional - English, Punjabi or Hindi)
  tag: Free                                    (optional - a small label on the card)

Example (remove the spaces before ### to use it):
    ### Book | Typing Basics for Exams
    url: https://example.com/typing-basics.pdf
    desc: Home row to speed building, with practice tables.
    language: English

    ### Video | Home Row in 10 Minutes
    url: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XXXXXXXXXXX
    desc: Recorded class
    language: Punjabi

Anything above the first ### line is ignored. The links open in the student's browser.
